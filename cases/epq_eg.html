How we turned lead generation into a trackable operation

Growth strategy, product marketing and agentic AI working together so no lead is lost after the ad.

The problem wasn't just in the ad

Generating a lead isn't enough when the operation loses speed or context right after it arrives. The ad did its part. What came next needed structure.

  • Customer service heavily dependent on manual operation
  • Information scattered across channels and people
  • Real difficulty tracking each lead through the funnel
  • No structured follow-up routine
  • Recurring objections without a standardized response
  • Little visibility into what actually happened in customer service

Generating the lead was only the beginning. The challenge was not losing it afterwards.

The answer

From lead to next step

The answer wasn't a new tool. It was an operational layer connecting marketing, customer service, data and automation into a single trackable flow — supported by three pillars working together.

01 Ad → 02 Lead → 03 Triage → 04 Customer service → 05 Follow-up → 06 Enrollment

01 · Growth

Funnel structuring, lead analysis, customer service tracking, temperature classification and creation of a follow-up logic.

02 · Product Marketing

Understanding the real doubts and objections of leads to turn that information into more adequate communication, scripts, offers and campaigns.

03 · Agentic AI

Use of AI and automations to execute operational tasks, generate briefings, organize information, track processes and support the continuous evolution of the operation.

Growth in practice

From concept to process

Customer service

Script built from real objections

The customer service playbook wasn't born from assumption: it was built on the doubts and objections that actually appeared in conversations, to give consistency to the response — without making it rigid for the person serving.

Follow-up

Customer service doesn't end when the price is sent

The goal was to give commercial continuity to the lead without turning follow-up into spam.

  • D+1 — Breaking the silence: First return after a period without response.
  • D+3 — Content / value: Useful information, not a chase.
  • D+4 — Real urgency + offer: Reinforcing the offer with a concrete reason to decide.
  • D+8 — Pattern break: A different approach from the previous ones.
  • D+12 — Reactivation: Last structured attempt before closing the cycle.
